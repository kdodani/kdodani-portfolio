# Writing Style Guide

Tone:
- thoughtful
- modern
- strategic
- concise
- product-led
- human-centered

Writing should feel:
- intelligent but approachable
- analytical without sounding academic
- modern and design-aware

Avoid:
- buzzwords
- generic PM language
- overly corporate tone
- sounding overly technical

Prefer:
- concise insights
- systems thinking
- user psychology
- workflow design
- adoption language
- behavioral framing

The writing should resemble:
- Figma
- Notion
- Linear
- Stripe
- modern AI SaaS companies
