# Portfolio Hero Section

## Goal
This hero section should position Khushboo Dodani as a modern product manager focused on:
- growth
- UX and behavioral design
- platform/product systems
- AI-native workflows
- intelligent decision support systems

The tone should feel:
- concise
- confident
- thoughtful
- modern
- technically literate without sounding overly technical
- credible for both startups and larger product-led tech companies

Avoid:
- buzzword-heavy AI language
- sounding corporate or resume-like
- generic “passionate product manager” phrasing
- healthcare-only positioning

The messaging should signal:
- systems thinking
- product intuition
- adoption/growth mindset
- AI-native product fluency
- experience working in complex regulated environments
