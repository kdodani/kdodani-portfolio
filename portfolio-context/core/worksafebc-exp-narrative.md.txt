# WorkSafeBC Experience Narrative

This section should not read like a traditional resume.

The goal is to show progression from:
- workflow optimization
- operational UX
- platform modernization
- ecosystem coordination
- AI-native systems thinking

The experience section itself should function as both:
- experience
- skills evolution

The reader should feel increasing:
- systems thinking
- abstraction
- platform depth
- orchestration complexity
- AI fluency

The tone should feel:
- thoughtful
- editorial
- systems-oriented
- calm
- strategic

NOT:
- corporate
- buzzword-heavy
- startup hype
- feature-shipping focused

Each experience block contains:
1. Narrative intro
2. Focus areas
3. How my thinking evolved
4. Capabilities developed
5. Selected launches

Launch cards should be:
- concise
- high-signal
- curiosity-driven

NOT mini case studies.

Card structure:
- title
- 1-line positioning statement
- 1 short supporting sentence
- tags

The WorkSafeBC section should position me as:
“A PM who evolved into designing intelligent operational systems inside regulated environments.”

Key themes:
- workflow orchestration
- operational systems
- platform modernization
- AI-assisted workflows
- recommendation systems
- API ecosystems
- identity systems
- trust-critical UX
- ecosystem integrations
- human-in-the-loop AI
