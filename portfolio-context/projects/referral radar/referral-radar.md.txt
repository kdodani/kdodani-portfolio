# Referral Radar - Project Context

## Project Overview

Referral Radar was an AI-powered referral recommendation system built during WorkSafeBC's InnoFest 2025 innovation challenge.

The challenge was organized for the Healthcare Programs department and focused on the following question:

> How can we utilize AI to automate tasks, enhance provider collaboration, and improve program measurement?

36 ideas were submitted across the organization.

Referral Radar was shortlisted as a finalist in the Process Transformation category and ultimately placed 2nd among the finalists.

The project was completed as a rapid innovation sprint with a cross-functional team consisting of product managers, data scientists, architects, and developers.

---

## Healthcare Programs Context

The Healthcare Programs department oversees approximately 70 contracted healthcare services across British Columbia including physiotherapy, counselling, rehabilitation, and long-term care.

The department coordinates healthcare services through external provider networks and supports injured workers throughout their recovery journey.

One of the challenges faced by case managers is identifying appropriate healthcare programs and referrals from large volumes of claim information distributed across notes, documents, assessments, and communications.

---

## Problem

Case managers review large amounts of claim information to determine when an injured worker may benefit from a healthcare program or referral.

Relevant information is often buried inside:

* Intake notes
* Claim documents
* Medical reports
* Counselling plans
* Case manager notes
* Return-to-work planning documents

As claim complexity increases, opportunities for early intervention or referral may be missed.

The team explored whether AI could proactively identify referral opportunities and explain its reasoning using claim evidence.

---

## Solution

Referral Radar was designed as an AI-assisted referral recommendation engine.

The system analyzes claim information and surfaces:

* Recommended healthcare programs
* Referral priority levels
* Supporting evidence from claim documents
* Claim timelines
* Searchable claim insights
* Visual relationship mapping between issues, interventions, and outcomes

The goal was not to replace case managers but to help them identify referral opportunities faster and with greater confidence.

### Synthetic Data Creation

Because production claim data could not be used during the innovation challenge, realistic training and testing data needed to be created.

The healthcare SME supporting the project had limited availability.

To overcome this constraint:

* I used ChatGPT to generate realistic claim conversations and case management scenarios.
* Created multiple versions of injured worker and case manager interactions.
* Worked directly with an experienced case manager to review and validate scenarios.
* Incorporated feedback and revisions.
* Approved scenarios were provided to the data science team for model development and testing.

This allowed the team to rapidly create realistic evaluation data while respecting privacy and security requirements.

---

### UX and Experience Design

The initial concepts focused primarily on recommendation lists and traditional AI outputs.

I pushed the team to create a more interactive and explainable experience.

The goal was to help users understand:

* Why a recommendation was generated
* What evidence supported it
* How claim events were connected

This led to the creation of an interactive visual mind map experience rather than a simple recommendation screen.

The mind map became one of the most memorable parts of the demonstration.

## Key Features

### Referral Recommendations

AI-generated referral recommendations ranked by priority level.

Examples:

* Trauma-Informed Counselling
* Return-to-Work Planning
* Vocational Rehabilitation Intake

Each recommendation included supporting evidence.

---

### Evidence Traceability

Users could inspect the source information behind recommendations.

Examples included:

* Relevant claim notes
* Counselling plans
* Medical assessments
* Supporting snippets

This helped improve transparency and explainability.

---

### Claim Timeline

Generated a summarized timeline of important claim events.

Examples:

* Initial assessments
* Medical updates
* Recovery milestones
* Referral activities

---

### Search Experience

Users could search claim documents and notes using natural language.

---

### Interactive Mind Map

Visualized relationships between:

* Symptoms
* Barriers
* Healthcare programs
* Recommendations
* Claim events

The visualization allowed users to explore connections rather than reading through large volumes of text.

---

### Agent Trace View

Displayed how the AI system arrived at recommendations and decisions.
