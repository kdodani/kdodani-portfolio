Workplace Injury Claim Timeline

Claim ID: #12345 | Duration: December 2023 - September 2024
Claimant: Sarah | Injury: Fall down stairs → Torn meniscus + Post-trauma psychological impact
Phase 1: Initial Incident
December 18, 2023
Incident Report & Intake Call
Sarah falls down 30 steps at work in front of colleagues. Reports swollen knee and emotional distress from the public nature of the incident. Jennifer (claims adjuster) demonstrates empathy and holistic approach from the start.
Phase 2: Medical Treatment
January 5, 2024
MRI Results & Surgery Booking
MRI reveals torn meniscus. Surgery scheduled for January 20. Sarah expresses uncertainty about returning to work, hinting at emotional concerns beyond physical injury.
Phase 3: Physical Recovery
February - March 2024
Post-Surgery Recovery & Return to Work
Physical healing progresses, but Sarah reports feeling like "the girl who fell." Develops avoidance behaviors, taking alternate routes to avoid the incident location. Early signs of psychological impact emerge.
Phase 3: Ongoing Issues
April 2024
Mid-Claim Check-In
Knee is healing well, but Sarah reports morning dread, chest tightness, and social isolation. Jennifer recognizes these as important signals requiring attention beyond physical recovery.
Phase 4: Mental Health Recognition
May 18, 2024
Turning Point - Mental Health Referral
Jennifer acknowledges Sarah's psychological symptoms as legitimate and refers her for mental health assessment. Sarah accepts the referral, recognizing her need for support.
Phase 4: AI Detection
May 19, 2024
AI Insight Aggregation
Referral Radar Engine analyzes case notes and identifies key indicators: "humiliating fall," avoidance behaviors, and panic symptoms. Recommends psychology assessment.
Phase 4: Professional Assessment
May 22 - June 12, 2024
Psychology Assessment & CBT Recommendation
Dr. Emily C. conducts assessment and confirms post-incident trauma. Recommends Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) program. Mental Health Advisor approves enrollment in specialized return-to-work program.
Phase 4: Program Enrollment
July 12 - Late July 2024
Mental Health Recovery Pathway
Claim plan updated to include mental health component. Sarah enrolled in Workplace Mental Health Recovery Pathway with trauma-informed therapy, flexible coaching, and confidence-building support.
Phase 4: Therapeutic Progress
Mid-August 2024
First Mental Health Check-In
Sarah reports positive progress with therapy, gaining insight into her emotional response to the incident. Begins verbalizing and processing feelings of embarrassment and trauma.
Phase 5: Recovery & Closure
Early September 2024
Milestone Achievement
Sarah successfully uses the main stairs for the first time since her fall, demonstrating significant psychological recovery and reclaiming of the workplace space.
Phase 5: Claim Closure
Mid-September 2024
Successful Claim Resolution
Both physical and mental health recovery complete. Claim closed with ongoing support resources available through employer. Sarah expresses gratitude for comprehensive care approach.

Key Insights & Best Practices

Holistic Approach

Claims adjuster recognized early that recovery involved both physical and emotional components, leading to comprehensive care.

AI-Assisted Detection

Technology helped identify psychological indicators that might have been missed, enabling timely intervention.

Trauma-Informed Care

Specialized mental health programs addressed workplace-specific psychological impacts, not just general therapy.

Patient-Centered Communication

Empathetic, non-judgmental communication built trust and encouraged openness about psychological symptoms.

Flexible Treatment Planning

Claim plan adapted to include mental health components when traditional physical recovery proved insufficient.

Meaningful Outcomes

Success measured not just by physical healing but by behavioral milestones and workplace reintegration confidence.
