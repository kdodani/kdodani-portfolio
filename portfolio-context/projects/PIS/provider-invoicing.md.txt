# Project Name

Provider Invoice Submission Modernization

# Project Summary

Led a digital adoption initiative focused on migrating healthcare providers from paper/fax invoice workflows to a streamlined digital experience.

The project focused on reducing friction, improving activation, and driving behavior change through workflow simplification and targeted adoption strategies.

# The Problem

Providers continued using paper and fax workflows despite digital alternatives existing.

The core problem was not only usability — it was behavior change and adoption resistance.

# User Pain Points

- Paper workflows felt familiar
- Digital workflows created friction
- Existing submission flows were too complex
- Providers did not perceive enough value in switching as switching would increase their workflow steps

# My Role

- Led product strategy
- Conducted workflow and UX analysis
- Identified provider segmentation patterns
- Prioritized UX improvements
- Partnered with marketing and communications teams
- Supported adoption strategy

# Product Thinking

Key insight:
A small number of providers drove a large percentage of paper volume.

This led to:
- segmentation strategy
- workflow simplification
- targeted interventions
- adoption-focused roadmap decisions

# UX & Workflow Improvements

- Reduced workflow complexity
- Reduced cognitive load
- Improved editing experience
- Improved submission efficiency
- Simplified invoice management
